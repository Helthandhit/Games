This Privacy Policy explains how Health and HIT ("we", "us") handles information in Game Hub and the games it contains (PaddockScan, Health and HIT, and Sprinkles and Layers), whether played in a browser or installed as an Android app (the "Games").

1. Summary

We do not collect, store or share any personal data. The Games have no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

2. Information stored on your device

Your game progress and settings (for example coins, levels, unlocked cars, high scores and sound/graphics preferences) are saved only on your device, using your browser's or the app's local storage. This data never leaves your device and we cannot access it. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or your browser's site data, or by uninstalling the app.

3. Sharing feature

Some games let you share a score card. When you tap Share, the Games use your device's built-in share sheet. You choose which app (if any) receives the content and what is sent. We do not receive or store anything you share. Content you share with other apps is governed by those apps' privacy policies.

4. No third-party analytics or advertising

The Games contain no third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or social media SDKs and do not use cookies for tracking. The Android app works fully offline and requests no special permissions such as location, contacts, camera or microphone. If you play in a web browser, the website host (for example GitHub Pages or our web host) may automatically log standard technical data such as IP addresses for security and operational purposes under its own privacy policy. We do not use this data to identify you.

5. Children's privacy

The Games are not directed at children under 13, and some content (a comedic, fictional "steroids" joke) is intended for teens and adults. Because we collect no personal data from anyone, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has somehow provided personal information to us (for example by email), contact us and we will delete it.

6. Contacting us by email

If you email us, we will use your email address and message only to reply to you and will delete them when no longer needed. We do not sell or share them.

7. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have rights to access, correct and delete personal data held about you. As we hold no data from gameplay, these will normally only apply to emails you have sent us.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be on this page with its "Last updated" date.
